EMPILE - PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026

## Overview
Empile is a casual puzzle game. Your privacy matters to us.

## Data Collection
Empile collects minimal data:

### Local Data (Stored on Your Device Only)
- Game scores and high scores
- Game settings (sound, haptics)
- Game progress and statistics
- These are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere

### Online Services
- Game Center: Optional. If you enable it, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center service to compete with friends
- Ads: The game may show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect:
  - Approximate location (based on IP)
  - Device information
  - Advertising ID
  - See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions
Empile requests NO special permissions. It doesn't access:
- Camera or microphone
- Photos or files
- Contacts
- Health data
- Location (except via ads)

## Third-Party Services
- **Apple Game Center**: For leaderboards and achievements (optional)
- **Google AdMob**: For advertisements
- **Apple App Store**: For crash reports (if enabled in settings)

## Children's Privacy
Empile is suitable for all ages. It contains:
- No personal data collection from children
- Age-appropriate casual gameplay
- No external links or social features
- No in-app purchases requiring payment details

## Data Retention
- Game data is stored locally on your device
- Game Center data is managed by Apple
- You can reset all game data anytime in the app settings
