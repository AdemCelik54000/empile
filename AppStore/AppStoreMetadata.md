## Description (App Store - 4000 chars max)

Empile is a captivating puzzle game where you stack blocks higher and higher. Simple mechanics, endless challenge.

**HOW TO PLAY**
- Tap the screen to grab the waiting block
- Drag to aim, release to stack it
- Hold your finger and tap with a second finger to rotate
- Stack blocks carefully to build the tallest tower

**KEY FEATURES**
✦ Addictive stacking gameplay
✦ Colorful, vibrant visuals with dynamic motes floating through the sky
✦ Smart wind physics that affects your tower
✦ Multiple block types with unique properties:
  - Stone: Reliable and stable
  - Spring: Launches blocks skyward (use carefully!)
  - Glass: Fragile but worth points
  - Gold: Precious anchors for tall towers
✦ Milestone rewards that intensify gameplay
✦ Power-ups to save your tower:
  - Reshape: Change block form
  - Rematerial: Switch block type
  - Grapple: Pull buried blocks free
  - Extra Life: Get another chance
✦ Birds visit to perch on your tower
✦ Beautiful blend of strategy and luck
✦ Global leaderboards via Game Center

**THE CHALLENGE**
Every tower is unique. Wind gusts test your stability. Precise stacking matters. One mistake and gravity wins.

**PERFECT FOR**
Anyone who loves puzzle games, casual gaming, or a satisfying challenge. Play for 5 minutes or 50 – Empile adapts to your style.

No ads in the main game. Optional ads unlock power-ups.
